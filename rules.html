Robot Game Rules

🎮 Basic Rules

  • Control your robot using W/A/S/D keys
  • Stay within the designated game area
  • Avoid collisions with other robots
  • Complete challenges within the time limit

🎯 Objectives

  • Navigate through obstacles
  • Collect points by completing tasks
  • Work together with your team
  • Have fun while learning!

⚠️ Safety Guidelines

  • Keep robots at a safe speed
  • Maintain clear space around the game area
  • Follow instructor guidance
  • Handle equipment with care
